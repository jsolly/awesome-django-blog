<p style="margin-left:0;">These are the same dump-and-go Instant Pot recipes, redrawn as recipe summary tables (Cooking for Engineers / Tabular Recipe Notation). Amounts are in grams. Cook on high pressure with a full natural release unless a recipe says otherwise. Frozen chicken is 26 min. Sauté assumes 14–28 g oil.</p>

<h2 style="margin-left:0;"><strong>Things to Know</strong></h2>

<ul><li data-list-item-id="e89d01970da854dbd121d488d98bebf6a"><p style="margin-left:0;"><strong>HP</strong> = High Pressure. Full natural release (the pin drops on its own) unless a recipe says Quick Release.</p></li><li data-list-item-id="e0388c09ccbdfc700e7d19cea01cb702d"><p style="margin-left:0;"><strong>Cook on high pressure</strong> — full natural release unless the cell says quick release. Frozen chicken default is 26 min.</p></li><li data-list-item-id="e5d87ae96a45380cbb4c686a53d350ecf"><p style="margin-left:0;"><strong>Sauté</strong> — 14–28 g canola oil unless the table names another fat.</p></li><li data-list-item-id="e4f75af2c00caf064af6ddb982b6e56b8"><p style="margin-left:0;"><strong>Blend</strong> — immersion blender in the pot (or a small blender).</p></li><li data-list-item-id="e5238376fbdecdcb1fff13e50f27c204a"><p style="margin-left:0;"><strong>Mix</strong> — if a step says a gram amount of mix, blend those spices in bulk and use that many grams.</p></li><li data-list-item-id="efea654899ad5c8f33c88a5ea45356f24"><p style="margin-left:0;"><strong>Quantities</strong> are in grams. Whole spices (bay leaves, cardamom pods, cinnamon stick) stay as counts. Inferred amounts are noted under the recipe.</p></li></ul>

<p style="margin-left:0;">Ingredients are rows, in the order they are first used. Each column to the right is a later step. A cell spans exactly the ingredient rows it acts on. Time reads left to right — the merge shape is the recipe.</p>
