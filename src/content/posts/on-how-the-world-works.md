The Following statements are not an ideal, nor do I believe they are unchangeable. I strive for a world that runs on love ❤️

"The world runs on canned responses and boilerplate masquerading as personal touches and custom work."

"The world runs on figuring out how valuable something is to you rather than how expensive it is to make."

"The world runs on being paid for your time to save someone else's time"

"The world runs on people wanting you to go the hard way because they did"

"The world runs on either being in the right category or avoiding one"

"The world runs on reducing cost anywhere you won't notice"

"The world runs on money or whatever it proxies"

"The world runs on people doing everything in their power to do hard things the easy way"
