## Finding a Position

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.linkedin.com/feed/">LinkedIn! </a> \- Connect with recruiters and reach out to real people who work at the companies you are interested in. If you see a 2nd-degree connection, ask one of your 1sts for an introduction! Turn on job alerts!

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.indeed.com">Indeed</a> - The 'God' of job postings. Most other job sites are simply copy/paste postings from Indeed. Turn on job alerts!

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://app.prowritingaid.com/GJC.org">GJC.org</a> 

<blockquote><p>One of the most visited GIS/RS jobs site on the Internet. Started in 1992, the GJC has helped thousands of GIS/RS professionals find positions. With hundreds of positions in our database, the GJC has resources for both employers and those seeking jobs.</p></blockquote>

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://app.prowritingaid.com/governmentjobs.com">governmentjobs.com</a> - Great for finding local and national government positions.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.usajobs.gov/">USAjobs</a> - Official government job focused on federal government jobs.

## Non-verbals/Appearance

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.photofeeler.com/">https://www.photofeeler.com/</a> - See how your profile photo rates in terms of ‘smart’, ‘trustworthy’, and ‘influential’

What the Body is saying - Navaro \[Book\] - Great book on non-verbals from an ex-FBI agent whose responsibility was to find liars.

## Researching a Company

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.glassdoor.com/index.htm">Glassdoor</a> - The ‘Yelp’ of companies. I always check GD for employee reviews before applying to a company.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.crunchbase.com/">Crunchbase</a> - Discover lots of info, including financials, acquisitions, news articles, and more!

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.bbb.org/">Better Business Bureau</a>

<blockquote><p>BBB’s mission is to be the leader in advancing marketplace trust. We do this by</p><p>Setting standards for marketplace trust</p><p>Encouraging and supporting best practices by engaging with and educating consumers and businesses</p><p>Celebrating marketplace role models</p><p>Calling out and addressing substandard marketplace behavior</p><p>Creating a community of trustworthy businesses and charities</p></blockquote>

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://sec.report/CIK/0001543151/1#documents">SEC documents (Uber example)</a> - For public companies, check out their S-1/A SEC filing containing a volume of information. The SEC requires IPOs to provide documents publically to educate potential investors on how a company intends to grow and make money. Visit the last page of ‘documents’, for the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://sec.report/Document/0001193125-19-120759/">S-1/A</a> containing a bundle of company info.
