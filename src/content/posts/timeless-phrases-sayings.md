## Intro

Words can inspire, motivate, and offer insights into the human experience. In this blog post, I've compiled a collection of timeless phrases and sayings that capture essential truths and offer a unique perspective on life. Use these words as a tool for self-reflection and understanding the world around you. Read on for a dose of inspiration and enlightenment.

“If the bulk of people buying something can’t fully appreciate or care about good quality, it’s a waste of time and money to provide it”

\-John S

Positioning: When one party speaks for 80%+ of the conversation, padding with generalities, vacuous truths, and self-evident, obvious statements, they are seizing control and ownership of the conversation, positioning its participants and everyone’s understanding. As people nod along with a stream of clear, undeniable statements, misinformation and opinion can be injected, and the people listening often swallow it, unaware. Positioning makes the speaker seem like they have understanding and a foundation of truth their statements are built on. A shrewd dissection of this behavior actually reveals that everyone’s intelligence is insulted by obvious generalities and their time is wasted by someone who controls the conversation’s outcome by verbal bombardment.

Zeal without knowledge is fire without light.

<i>Actori incumbit onus probatio</i>

"On the plaintiff rests the proving". The burden of proof falls to the plaintiff, claimant, or petitioner.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_alteram_partem"><i><strong>Audi alteram partem</strong></i></a> <i><strong>or</strong></i> <i><strong>audiatur et altera pars</strong></i>

"Listen to the other side", or "let the other side be heard as well". Refers to the idea that one cannot be fairly judged unless the cases for and against them have been heard.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cogitationis_poenam_nemo_patitur"><i><strong>Cogitationis poenam nemo patitur</strong></i></a>

"No one suffers punishment for mere intent." A crime is only committed through some act, not through a mere thought.

<i><strong>Consensus facit legem</strong></i>

"Consensus makes law". Stipulates that when two or more persons arrive at a good faith agreement, the law will insist on that agreement being carried out.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consuetudo_pro_lege_servatur"><i><strong>Consuetudo pro lege servatur</strong></i></a>

"Custom is held as law." Where no laws apply to a given situation, the customs of the place and time will have the force of law.

<i><strong>Contra non valentem agere nulla</strong></i> <strong>[or </strong> <i><strong>non</strong></i><strong>] </strong> <i><strong>currit praescriptio</strong></i>

A <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statute_of_limitation">statute of limitation</a> does not run against those who cannot act; this is the basis of the American discovery rule limiting prescriptive limitation periods.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuius_est_solum_eius_est_usque_ad_coelum_et_ad_inferos"><i><strong>Cuius est solum eius est usque ad coelum et ad inferos</strong></i></a>

"Whoever's is the soil, all the way to heaven and to the depths is theirs." Used in reference to the rights of property owners to the air above, and land below, their property.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delegatus_non_potest_delegare"><i><strong>Delegatus non potest delegare</strong></i></a>

"That which has been delegated cannot delegate further."

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_minimis_non_curat_lex"><i><strong>De minimis non curat lex</strong></i></a>

"The law does not concern itself with the smallest things". There must be a minimal level of substance or impact in order to bring a legal action.

<i><strong>Dubia in meliorem partem interpretari debent</strong></i>

"Doubtful things should be interpreted in the best way." Often spoken as "to give the benefit of the doubt".

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dura_lex,_sed_lex"><i><strong>Dura lex, sed lex</strong></i></a>

"The <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law">law</a> \[is\] harsh, but \[it is\] the law." It follows from the principle of the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_law">rule of law</a> that even <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draco_(lawgiver)">draconian</a> laws must be followed and enforced; if one disagrees with the result, one must seek to change the law.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ei_incumbit_probatio_qui_dicit"><i><strong>Ei incumbit probatio qui dicit</strong></i></a>

"Proof lies on the one who asserts." The concept that one is innocent until proven guilty.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_factis_jus_oritur"><i><strong>Ex factis jus oritur</strong></i></a>

"The law arises from the facts." A principle in international law that one must take facts on the ground into account when considering the legality of certain kinds of questions.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expressio_unius_est_exclusio_alterius"><i><strong>Expressio unius est exclusio alterius</strong></i></a>

"The express mention of one thing excludes all others." When items are listed, anything not explicitly stated is assumed to not be included.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_turpi_causa_non_oritur_actio"><i><strong>Ex turpi causa non oritur actio</strong></i></a>

"From a dishonorable cause an action does not arise." A party cannot bring a legal action for consequences of his own illegal act.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat_justitia_et_pereat_mundus"><i><strong>Fiat justitia et pereat mundus</strong></i></a>

"Let there be justice, though the world perish." Often used as a motto, notably by <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_I,_Holy_Roman_Emperor">Ferdinand I, Holy Roman Emperor</a>.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat_justitia_ruat_caelum"><i><strong>Fiat justitia ruat caelum</strong></i></a>

"Let justice be done though the heavens fall." Also sometimes a motto, a legal maxim that justice must be done regardless of the result otherwise.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalia_specialibus_non_derogant"><i><strong>Generalia specialibus non derogant</strong></i></a>

"The general does not detract from the specific." Specifies that a certain matter of law be covered by the most specific laws pertaining, in the event that broader laws conflict with the specific one.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ignorantia_juris_non_excusat"><i><strong>Ignorantia juris non excusat</strong></i></a>

"Ignorance of the law is no excuse." Not knowing that one's actions are forbidden by the law is not a defense.

<i><strong>In claris non fit interpretatio</strong></i>

"In clear things no interpretation is made." When a rule is clearly intelligible, there is no need of proposing a (usually extensive) interpretation.

<i><strong>Inadimplenti non est adimplendum</strong></i>

"One has no need to respect his obligation if the counter-party has not respected his own." This is used in <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_law_(common_law)">civil law</a> to briefly indicate a principle (adopted in some systems) referred to as the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synallagmatic_contract">synallagmatic contract</a>.<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brocard_(law)#cite_note-3">[3]</a>

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter_arma_enim_silent_leges"><i><strong>Inter arma enim silent leges</strong></i></a>

"For among arms, the laws fall silent." A concept that during war, many illegal activities occur. Also taken to mean that in times of war, laws are suppressed, ostensibly for the good of the country.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iura_novit_curia"><i><strong>Iura novit curia</strong></i></a>

"The court knows the law." Concept that parties to a case do not need to define how the law applies to their case. The court is solely responsible for determining what laws apply.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iudex_non_calculat"><i><strong>Iudex non calculat</strong></i></a>

"The judge does not calculate." A principle that calculation errors made by the court do not invalidate the judgement on a technicality. Also taken to mean that the judge does not tally up the arguments of both sides and decide in favor of the more numerous, but rather weighs all of the evidence without regard to the number of arguments made.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leges_humanae_nascuntur,_vivunt,_moriuntur"><i><strong>Leges humanae nascuntur, vivunt, moriuntur</strong></i></a>

"The laws of man are born, live, and die." Illustrates that laws are made, are in force for a period, and then become obsolete.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lex_posterior_derogat_priori"><i><strong>Lex posterior derogat priori</strong></i></a>

"Later law removes the earlier." More recent law overrules older ones on the same matter.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lex_retro_non_agit"><i><strong>Lex retro non agit</strong></i></a>

"The law does not operate retroactively." A law cannot make something illegal that was legal at the time it was performed. See <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_post_facto_law">ex post facto law</a>.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lex_specialis_derogat_legi_generali"><i><strong>Lex specialis derogat legi generali</strong></i></a>

"A law governing a specific subject matter overrides a law which only governs general matters."

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maleficia_propositis_distinguuntur"><i><strong>Maleficia propositis distinguuntur</strong></i></a>

"Evil acts are distinguished from (evil) purposes"<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brocard_(law)#cite_note-4">[4]</a> or "crimes are distinguished by evil intent".<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brocard_(law)#cite_note-5">[5]</a>

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nemo_auditur_propriam_turpitudinem_allegans"><i><strong>Nemo auditur propriam turpitudinem allegans</strong></i></a>

"No one can be heard, who invokes his own guilt." Nobody can bring a case that stems from their own illegal act.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nemo_dat_quod_non_habet"><i><strong>Nemo dat quod non habet</strong></i></a>

"No one gives what they do not have."<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brocard_(law)#cite_note-6">[6]</a> The basic rule that a person who does not own property (e.g. a thief) cannot confer it on another except with the true owner's authority (i.e. as his agent). Exceptions to this rule include sales under statutory powers, and cases where the doctrine of <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estoppel">estoppel</a> prevents a legal owner from denying a seller's right to sell.

<i><strong>Nemo debet esse iudex in propria</strong></i>

"No one shall be a judge in his own case." In the past it was thought that it included just two rules, namely (1) <i>nemo debet esse iudex in propria causa</i> (no one shall be a judge in his own case).

<i><strong>Nemo ius ignoratur censetur</strong></i>

"Not knowing the law is harmful." Everyone should know the law. This is used in European Law-countries with a history of Roman law; the 'sentence' was first made by <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotle">Aristotle</a>.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nemo_judex_in_sua_causa"><i><strong>Nemo judex in sua causa</strong></i></a>

"No one shall be a judge in his own case." Prevents conflict of interest in courts. Often invoked when there is really no conflict, but when there is even the appearance of one.

<i><strong>Nemo plus iuris ad alium transferre potest quam ipse habet</strong></i>

"No one can transfer a greater right than he himself has." A purchaser of stolen goods will not become the rightful owner thereof, since the seller himself was not the owner to begin with.

<i><strong>Non faciat malum, ut inde veniat bonum</strong></i>

"Not to do evil that good may come." Performing some illegal action is not excused by the fact that a positive result came therefrom. Often used to argue that some forms of expression, such as graffiti or pornographic films, cannot be given the protection of law (e.g. copyright) as they are or may be considered illegal or morally reprehensible.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nulla_poena_sine_lege"><i><strong>Nulla poena sine lege</strong></i></a> <i><strong>or</strong></i> <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nullum_crimen,_nulla_poena_sine_praevia_lege_poenali"><i><strong>nullum crimen, nulla poena sine praevia lege poenali</strong></i></a>

"No penalty without law" or "no crime, no punishment without a previous penal law". (1) One cannot be prosecuted for doing something that is not prohibited by law. (2) One cannot be prosecuted for doing something that was not prohibited by law at the time and place it was committed, notwithstanding laws made since that time. A form of prohibition on retroactive laws.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacta_sunt_servanda"><i><strong>Pacta sunt servanda</strong></i></a>

"Agreements are to be kept." Contracts are the law <i>or</i> contracts establish obligations (between those who sign them).

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par_in_parem_non_habet_imperium"><i><strong>Par in parem non habet imperium</strong></i></a>

"Equals have no sovereignty over each other."

<i><strong>Prior tempore potior iure</strong></i>

"Earlier in time, stronger in law." A legal principle that older laws take precedence over newer ones. Another name for this principle is <i>lex posterior</i>.

<i><strong>Prius quam exaudias ne iudices</strong></i>

"Before you hear, do not judge."

<i><strong>Probatio vincit praesumptionem</strong></i>

"Proof overcomes presumption."

<i><strong>Prout patet per recordum</strong></i>

"As appears in the record". Used to cite something that has already been admitted into the record. It was frequently used in <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plea">pleadings</a>, generally abbreviated "prout &amp;c.", to indicate that a fact was supported by documentary evidence. Failure to use this phrase correctly could be a fatal defect and so cause a case to fail.<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brocard_(law)#cite_note-7">[7]</a>

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qui_facit_per_alium_facit_per_se"><i><strong>Qui facit per alium facit per se</strong></i></a>

"Who acts through another, acts himself." One who delegates a task to another, takes full responsibility for the performance of that act as if he himself had done it. Basis for the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_agency">law of agency</a>.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Quod_est_necessarium_est_licitum&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1"><i><strong>Quod est necessarium est licitum</strong></i></a>

"What is necessary is lawful."

<i><strong>Quod non est in registro, non est in Mundo</strong></i>

"What is not in the register is not in the world." What is not reported in the (related, referring) registry, has no legal relevance. Used when a formal act (usually a recording or a transcription) is required in order to give consistence, content or efficacy to a <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right">right</a>.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Res_ipsa_loquitur"><i><strong>Res ipsa loquitur</strong></i></a>

"The thing speaks for itself." Used in tort law when there is no proof of what caused the harm, but it is most likely only the thing that could have caused the harm.

<i><strong>Res inter alios acta vel iudicata, aliis nec nocet nec prodocet</strong></i>

What has been agreed/decided between people (a specific group) can neither benefit nor harm a third party (meaning: two or more people cannot agree amongst each other to establish an obligation for a third party who was not involved in the negotiation; furthermore, any benefit that may be established will have to be accepted by the third party before it can be implemented).

<i><strong>Rex non potest peccare</strong></i>

"The king can do no wrong." Used to describe the basis for <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign_immunity">sovereign immunity</a>.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salus_populi_suprema_lex_esto"><i><strong>Salus populi suprema lex esto</strong></i></a>

"The good of the people shall be the supreme law." Used variously as a motto, a reminder, or a notion of how the law and governments in general should be.

<i><strong>Sententia quae in rem iudicatam transit, pro veritate habetur</strong></i>

When a definitive <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentence_(law)">sentence</a> is declared, it is considered to be the truth. In the case of a sentence <i>in rem iudicatam</i> (that finally consents to consider a judgement completed), its content will then be the only legally relevant consideration of a fact.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic_utere_tuo_ut_alienum_non_laedas"><i><strong>Sic utere tuo ut alienum non laedas</strong></i></a>

"Use your property so as not to injure that of your neighbours." While an individual is entitled to the use and enjoyment of one's estate, the right is not without limits. Restrictions can give rise to tort actions including trespass, negligence, and nuisance.<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brocard_(law)#cite_note-8">[8]</a>

<i><strong>Solve et repete</strong></i>

"Solve and resume." Respect your obligation first, then you can ask for reimbursement. Used in those situations in which one of the two (or more) parties needs to complete his obligation before being allowed to ask for the opposite obligation to be respected by his counter party. Usually this principle is used in fields and subjects in which a certain general steadiness or uniformity of the system has been considered a relevant value by the legislator. The case is typical of service contracts with repeated obligations (like with gas, water, electricity providers and similars), in which irregularities on one side cannot be balanced if not in a regular situation (i.e., of payments) on the other side. The customer, for example, might be asked to pay regularly the new bill, before contesting the previous one in which he found irregular calculations, and asking for a balancement with newer bills; he thus cannot by himself determine a discount in the next payment.

<i><strong>Ubi lex voluit, dixit; ubi noluit, tacuit</strong></i>

"When the law wills, it speaks; when it does not will, it is silent." When the law wants to regulate a matter in further detail, it does regulate the matter; when it does not want to regulate a matter in further detail, it remained silent (in the interpretation of a law, an excessively expansive interpretation might perhaps go beyond the intention of the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislator">legislator</a>, thus we must adhere to what is in the text of the law and draw no material consequences from the law's silence).

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra_posse_nemo_obligatur"><i><strong>Ultra posse nemo obligatur</strong></i></a>

"No one is obligated (to do) more than he can." Specifies that one should do what he can to support the community, but since everyone has different levels of ability, it cannot be expected that all will perform the same.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laches_(equity)"><i><strong>Vigilantibus non dormientibus aequitas subvenit</strong></i></a>

"Equity aids the vigilant, not the sleeping." Concept that if an opposing party unreasonably delays bringing an action, that it is no longer considered just to hear their claim, due to fundamental changes in circumstance brought on by their delay.

<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volenti_non_fit_injuria"><i><strong>Volenti non fit injuria</strong></i></a>

"Injury is not done to the willing." Notion that a person cannot bring a claim against another for injury, if said person willingly placed themselves in a situation where they knew injury could result.
