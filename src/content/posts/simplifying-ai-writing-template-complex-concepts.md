AI prompts can often seem like complex and mysterious processes. I've broken them into essential components to better understand these concepts. The following is a list of critical elements that make up a piece of writing, designed to be as broad and general as possible to be applied to various writing subjects.

## Aspects of Writing

<ul style="list-style-type:disc;"><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Level of Detail (Detailed, Concise)</li><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Length (One sentence, four Paragraphs)</li><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Structure (Haiku, poem, essay, Article, short story, novella)</li><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Setting (Small Town, Post-Apocalyptic Wasteland, A Magical Forest)</li><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Genre (Action, Romance, Drama, Western, Journalistic Writing)</li><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Story elements (Conflict, suspense, resolution, climax, setting)</li><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Word Choice (Idioms, Figures of Speech, Slang, Jargon, Imagery, metaphors, similes, dialog, rhyme, alliteration)</li><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Perspective (First-person, second-person, third-person, omniscient, limited omniscient, objective)</li><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Pacing (Fast, Slow, variable)</li><li style="list-style-type:disc;">Tone (Humorous, Inspiring, Professional, Casual, Mocking, Persuasive)</li></ul>

<h2 style="list-style-type:disc;">Using The Template</h2>

Here's a prompt I am using when I am using ChatGPT for writing.

<blockquote><p>Write a [Length], [Pacing] paced, [Level of Detail], and [Tone] [Structure] in the [Genre] genre set in a [Setting] with [Story Elements] and [Ammount] [Word Choice] from the [Point of View] point of view. [Story Prompt]</p></blockquote>

## A Short Story

<blockquote><p>Write a four-paragraph, fast paced, detailed, and humorous short story in the action genre set in a post-apocalyptic wasteland with no resolution and a lot of slang from the omniscient point of view. The story follows a survivor named Jake who is down to his last 6oz of water.</p></blockquote>

## Professional Writing

<blockquote><p>Write a three-paragraph, even-paced concise and professional article in the geospatial genre about quantitative analysis with metaphors and idoims from the first-person point of view. The article is about the benefits of using a GIS.</p></blockquote>

## Conclusion

Working with a template is a great way to get the most out of GPT's capabilities. By providing GPT with a specific structure and guidelines, I can have more control over the final output. Instead of leaving it up to GPT to decide on the tone, pacing, and other variables, I can specify them explicitly. This way, I can ensure that the final output aligns with my intended style and purpose. Additionally, using a template can also save time by reducing the need for editing and revisions. It can also be helpful in situations where you want to maintain consistency within a series of texts, for example, an article series or a book. Overall, a template can be a powerful tool for creating high-quality and coherent text with GPT.
